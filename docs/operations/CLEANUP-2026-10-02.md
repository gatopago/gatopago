### Why the previous UI said “unavailable”

Migration and backend capabilities had been presented alongside consumer screens
that were not connected to a complete V3 flow. A contract or passing unit test
does not connect checkout, Earn, swaps or cross-chain execution to the web app.
Blanket notices also understated the account and transfer components that do
exist. This cleanup removes empty screens and describes specific limitations;
it does not relabel unimplemented functionality as working. Real auth/config,
network and evidence failures must still be shown.
